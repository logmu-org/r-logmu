# logmu 0.1.0

Initial CRAN release.

- `exp_data` and `val_data` represent mortality experience and liability 
  valuation data in a data form-like format.
- `aev` captures actual, expected and variance deaths values and associated 
  statistics.
- The S3 class `mortality` with specialised sub-types `mortality_table` and 
  `mortality_const` enable users to specify mortality for individuals.
